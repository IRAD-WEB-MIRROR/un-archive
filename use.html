Using the archive

Anyone may listen in the reading room. You don’t need to be a student or a scholar.

Reading room
National Conservatory of Folk Music, University Street 11, Talas, second floor, room 214
Open
Tuesday and Thursday 10:00–17:00, Saturday 10:00–14:00. Closed in August.
Bring
An identity document. Headphones are provided.
Copies
For families of the singers: free. For research: a listening copy on request. For broadcast, film or a record: written permission from the singer or the family first; the archive helps you find them.

How to cite

Give the entry number, the title, the collector, the place and the year: Ün II.412, Lament for a brother, recorded by Aigul Choro kyzy, Suusamyr 1987.

Ask for a recording

Listening evenings

On the last Thursday of the month (the third in December) at 18:00 the keeper plays an hour of reels in the reading room, with tea. Free; no need to book. Not in June, July or August.

Families

If a singer in the archive is your mother, grandfather or neighbour, please tell us. We will send you the recording, and if you would rather it was not online, it comes down the same week.
