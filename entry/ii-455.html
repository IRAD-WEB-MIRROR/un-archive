Lament of the bride leaving

Sabira Ermek kyzy and two neighbours; recorded by Nurgul Abdrakhmanova

0:00 / 4:48

The listening copy of this reel is in the reading room. Online listening opens volume by volume as the families agree: see the listening room.

English working translation by the archive. The Iradi text is in the reading-room catalogue.

1Mother, the felt you made me is on their camel,2the spoon you cut me is in their bag,3only my voice I am leaving here, keep it on the hook.

3 The two neighbours answer each line with the refrain ai-ai, not transcribed.
