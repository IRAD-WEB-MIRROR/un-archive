Song of the felt-rolling

Women of the Bakyt collective farm; recorded by Sagyn Jusupbekov

0:00 / 5:34

The listening copy of this reel is in the reading room. Online listening opens volume by volume as the families agree: see the listening room.

English working translation by the archive. The Iradi text is in the reading-room catalogue.

1Roll it, roll it, the wool is from my father's sheep,2roll it, roll it, the pattern is my mother's horns,3roll it until it is one thing and nobody can pull it apart.

2 ‘Horns’ is the ram's-horn pattern, kochkor muuz.
