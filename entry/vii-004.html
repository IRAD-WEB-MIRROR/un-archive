Hymn of the morning, in three voices

Three men of the yurt church; recorded by Kubanychbek Asanov

0:00 / 6:02

The listening copy of this reel is in the reading room. Online listening opens volume by volume as the families agree.

English working translation by the archive. The Iradi text is in the reading-room catalogue.

1Light, come down the side of the mountain,2light, come into the felt house,3we are awake, we are three, we are singing.

1 bash ün alone; the orto ün enters at line 2 and the drone at line 3. 3 The high kekilik ün is heard above the last word.
