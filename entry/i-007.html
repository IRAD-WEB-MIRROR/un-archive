I.007Reel 2, side AChong-Talas, 196823 min 14 s

Janyl rides out from Chong-Talas

Asan Turdubek uulu, ırchy, with komuz, in the kolkhoz club; recorded by Aigul Choro kyzy and Sagyn Jusupbekov

0:00 / 23:14

The listening copy of this reel is in the reading room. Online listening opens volume by volume as the families agree: see the listening room.

English working translation by the archive. The Iradi text is in the reading-room catalogue.

1She saddled the grey with the white star at dawn,2the women of the camp stood with their hands at their mouths,3no one had seen a girl take the spear from the wall.4“If the men are asleep,” she said, “the horse is awake.”

2 The singer stops here to tune and starts the line again. 4 This line is not in the 1938 Talas text; Jusupbekov printed it as a variant in Volume III.
