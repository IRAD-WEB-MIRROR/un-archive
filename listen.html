Listening room

Nothing in the archive can be played online yet. Every recording belongs to the singer and the family, and we put a recording online only when they have said yes.

Of the 24 entries published here, 17 are digitised and wait for the families’ answer; the other 7 are still on the original tape, in the queue for the two machines. All of them can be heard in the reading room, room 214, on headphones at the listening table: on Tuesday and Thursday 10:00 to 17:00 and Saturday 10:00 to 14:00 (closed in August).

The first online volume will be Volume VII, Hymns in three voices, whose parishes have agreed in principle. When it opens, this page will play it.

Listening evenings

On the last Thursday of the month (the third in December) at 18:00 the keeper plays an hour of reels in the reading room, with tea. Free; no need to book. Not in June, July or August.
