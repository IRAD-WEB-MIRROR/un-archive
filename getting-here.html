Getting here

National Conservatory of Folk Music, University Street 11, Talas, second floor, room 214. Show your passport at the porter’s desk. Talas city buses: stop “University”. On the map.

Listening is free. Copies of recordings are made only with the family’s permission; ask the keeper.
